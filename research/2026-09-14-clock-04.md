## The chain

**Source finding:** Raban's and\naimol projection practice (recursion-01
chain, LUX-verified) treats the projected surface as part of the image, not
a window. The 1943 film itself interrupts with hard cuts and title cards.
Finding from the 2026-09-14 watch: at a cut, frame n shows the NEW scene
while frame n−180 shows the OLD one — the lag panel briefly contains a
DIFFERENT film. Evidence: /tmp/look-c03-a.png (credits ghosting under ramp
overlay).

**Artistic question:** What if the past is not painted ON the footage but
TORN OUT OF it — the memory band leaves the footage plane and hangs beside
it as a rigid shard, and the film's own cuts are what tear it loose?

**Material:** same Conquerb1943 cut (provenance clock-01-conquerb1943,
CC-PD). No new pulls.

**Three.js operation:** replace the flat wipe band (clock-03) with a
`pastShard` plane positioned by the DELAY ring content: seed the shard's
appearance at detected hard cuts (frame-difference threshold in the
existing ring write path, ~0 cost), then the shard drifts out along its
plane normal over ~1.5s and holds offset from the footage plane at a small
z increment. On reset the shard returns to the footage plane. Pointer
enumeration controls remain, but the raster flag panel from clock-03 is
REMOVED entirely.

**Observable results:** at each film cut, a visible shard separates from
the footage showing n−180 content; between cuts, the footage runs clean
with no overlay — removal made visible, i.e., the montage's own skeleton.
o11y: `clock04.shard {at, frame, source: 'cut'}`.

**Lineage:** branches clock-03 (`clock-03-b03234c`, unresolved): keeps the
1943 material and the delay ring; RETIRES the wipe band; removes the
pointer raster panel (subtraction discipline). Answers the open question
with a subtraction-first design — the gallery's interruption grammar is
now the film's.

## Requirement added during the 2026-09-14 dreaming check (AD dream: one-frame three-layer legibility)
At rest a viewer must be able to tell source, retained past, and a JUST-HAPPENED pointer cut apart without caption. The wipe currently vanishes after 700ms. The shard therefore stays displaced for a bounded window after a pointer cut (decay default ~6s, restored cleanly on the next reset), so the intervention leaves a visible resting trace. o11y: `clock04.intervention.decay {until}`.
