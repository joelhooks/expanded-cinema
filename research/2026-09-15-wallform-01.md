# wallform-01 — the past seats on a form (lane 2, Whitman Shower boundary)

Branch of colour-01@a5aabcd (live cut 2026-09-15T08:22Z).

## Question
Robert Whitman, *Shower* (1964): film projected onto a real shower stall
with water running — the image landing on a thing, not a flat plate. In
this room the withheld wall picture (withhold-01.3 @9cb8ac9) is a floating
card. Does the frozen past become more *present* when it has a body to
land on — a heap of shards, a cylinder segment, a draped sheet?

## The four (doctrine, AD seq-42/43)
- **Palette**: inherited from colour-01 (R 0xff2a1a / G 0x2bff6a /
  B 0x2a6aff + room amber 0xffa02a, Light Music 1975) — the form sits in
  the lit room, so colour continues across surfaces.
- **Light**: the same R/G/B + amber point lights carry onto the form; the
  form's relief is what makes the colour legible *as form* (each facet
  catches a different channel — visible handoff from plate to object).
- **Relief**: the form IS the relief study. Shard heap = ~40 thin box
  shards in a rough cone heap, frozen wall picture split across the shards'
  facets (one CanvasTexture, per-shard UV offsets). Cheapest strong relief;
  alternative (cylinder segment) would read as the existing screen.
- **TSL/operation**: the surface starts as the authored frozen frame
  (CanvasTexture), ONE node material carries it: colorNode samples the
  frozen picture with per-facet UV offsets + the 2x chroma mix retained.
  Operation = "the past is subdivided across a body". Video stays on the
  main screen; the wall picture alone moves onto the heap.

## Gate (self-read, then cut)
5s and 16s frames after the shutter: (a) frozen picture legible across the
shard facets, (b) facet shading shows light with a direction (relief
evident), (c) frames differ only if the heap settles/sways subtly — else
(c) = "wall picture moved from card to body" visible as a load-time
difference vs withhold-01.3. Attach frames; cut via /mcp pointer.
