# Study: browser-01 — browser as projector (chain before code; AD-sequence slot)

Direction: art-direction.svx 2026-09-13 "Browser as projector"
(Joel-approved): window resize is zoom, tab visibility is the shutter,
cursor is a body in the room that casts a shadow on the surface.

## The chain

**Source finding:** the lane's studies so far treat the browser as a
neutral window on a rendered world. The provocation makes the browser's
OWN mechanics the apparatus: resize, blur/focus, and cursor are already
physical events of a projection device — they only need to be claimed as
such and made measured.

**Artistic question:** if resize/visibility/cursor ARE the projector's
zoom, shutter, and body, does the viewer's ordinary browser behavior
become the film's editing — without any deliberate "interaction design"?

**Material:** the 1943 Conquerb1943 cut (provenance clock-01). No new
pulls.

**Three.js operation:** resize → dolly-zoom on the footage plane camera
(filter smoothed, target ∝ window height); visibilitychange → shutter:
opacity 0 with a mechanical beat on hide, half-beat on show (the past
panel stays visible for one beat after the shutter closes — the linger);
cursor → soft contact shadow following the pointer over the plane, with
the shadow two minutes late? No — the shadow cast by the cursor reveals
the PAST content under it (uses the existing DELAY ring sample).

**Observable results:** resizing visibly reframes without changing the
cut; tab-hide/show is a legible shutter with a past-linger beat; the
cursor drags a small window of past over the footage. o11y: `browser01
{event: resize|shutter|shadow, value}`.

**Lineage:** branches clock-02's ring grammar (delay sample reuse),
swaps the apparatus from time-of-film to time-of-browser. Distant to
twin-01 (rate) and clock-04 (split); closest lane cousin is the
"source, memory, intervention" three-layer claim — the cursor shadow is
a per-pixel intervention trace, already durable (it exists while the
cursor does). Headless-verifiable: resize/shadow events are synthetic-
drivable in the verification page; visibilitychange needs a browser but
not a phone.
