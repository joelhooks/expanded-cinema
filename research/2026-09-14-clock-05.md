## The chain

**Source finding:** a live public feed whose duration is the world's —
candidate: NOAA/NWS weather radar loops or a public harbour webcam with a
stable direct MP4/MJPEG URL. Constraint established by the ingest
investigation: the pipeline's archive-first policy is currently broken for
archive.org direct URLs (yt-dlp bug, flagged), so the study must pin a
stable URL + record fetch provenance per the research skill, and cache a
bounded snapshot server-side rather than depending on a fragile pull.

**Artistic question:** what does a delay field do to a picture that is
already late? The feed is the world's own delayed broadcast; the study's
DELAY ring adds a second lateness on top.

**Material:** live feed frames (public feed, license noted on the item
page), NOT the 1943 cut. Bounded snapshot for headless verification
(recorded frames replayed through the same ring code — same evidence path
as the phone-01 trace).

**Three.js operation:** reuse the clock-02 same-view ring capture (DELAY
visible as motion mismatch) but the delay baseline starts at the feed's
own latency, measured, not assumed: fetch two probes N seconds apart,
estimate wall-to-frame lag from a visible world clock in frame, make the
ring ride on top of that measured lateness.

**Observable results:** measured-lag receipt in o11y (`clock05.lag
{measured}`); visible double-lateness — the world's delay + the ring's
delay — on weather motion; stillness sections (no weather motion) show
only the ring's contribution.

**Lineage:** branches clock-02 (`clock-02-076405c`, retained): same
same-view ring grammar, new world material; the "clock you do not own" is
now literal — the study does not control the source's pace.
