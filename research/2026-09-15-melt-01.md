Source finding:    Liquid light shows (USCO, the Whitneys, Fillmore
                   projectionists like Glenn McKay) perturbed projected
                   footage with oil, water, and heat — the image's own
                   energy physically disturbed the medium showing it.
                   Jordan Belson's Allures (1961) / Vortex (1965) made
                   abstract light behave like a depth rather than a plane.
                   In this studio the equivalent perturbation is already
                   available: N-frame feedback. Sampling the render's own
                   recent frame and displacing form with it is the TSL
                   translation of what a crystal bowl of oil did with
                   projectors and a heat lamp — the source's motion
                   physically disturbs the surface carrying it.
Artistic question: Can stillness and motion become the whole study — the
                   film's luma drives a self-displacing relief that melts
                   under motion, heal under stillness, and detonate on
                   cuts — such that "does time read as liquid?" is answered
                   by the rendered surface, with no channel split, no
                   overlay, and no new effect appended?
Material:          The wallform base's live clip = Conquer by the Clock
                   (1943, archive.org, CC PD dedication) — provenance row
                   `clock-01-conquerb1943` in research/provenance.md,
                   sha256 02b596fb. One source, unchanged from the base it
                   branches from — the operation changes, not the footage.
Palette:           Source clip's own hues carried only by beam and fog;
                   fog colour nominally deep violet-grey ("smoke on the
                   R2 floor"), cited choice: Belson's fog-flooded deeps in
                   Allures. No additive accent colour — that would reopen
                   the paused colour lane.
Light:             The beam is the only lamp, as on the wallform base; fog
                   gains a body so the beam reads as a volume with colour
                   where it passes through the melt.
Geometry:          The wallform facet heap carried forward (instanced
                   facet relief, several-thousand instance budget already
                   proven at v5 d01f025 where facets began to carry the
                   picture). Change: facets stop being static shards and
                   become the voxels of the melt.
Node shader:       One TSL feedback-displacement material — vertex-level
                   displacement sampled from the panel's own frame buffer
                   N frames back, gain multiplies the live clip's luma.
                   This is the study's operation; nothing else is new.
Operation:         (1) render the relief to texture each frame;
                   (2) OFFSET-LATE texture lookup = the past;
                   (3) displace vertices along surface normal by
                   past-frame luma × gain × live-luma gate.
Duration:          Rail dwell unchanged from wallform; the tracking shot
                   now shows melt states, not just facet states.
Observable result: At the in-point: relief calm. As motion enters:
                   whole-field relief starts sliding; on a cut the relief
                   detonates to maximum displacement for the cut's
                   duration; on extended stillness the swarm settles back
                   toward flat. Three states visible to the rail.
Critique intents:  - Does melt heal cleanly, or does noise never leave?
                   - Does the melt read as the film being fluid, or as a
                   warp filter over a video? (latter = floor line 4 fail)
                   - Does the apparatus stay legible through max melt?
                   - Is the detonation-on-cut the razzle dazzle moment?
Lineage:           branches wallform-01 (facet heap + beam-only light):
                   keeps geometry, source, o11y chain; changes facet
                   behaviour static→feedback-melt via one TSL material.
Variable ladder:   v01 fixed N=8, gain floor 1.0 — prove melt reads at all
                   → v02 gain sweep (does detonation need more?) → v03 N
                   varies across surface (left = now, right = two seconds
                   ago) — the provocation's gradient as lens on the melt.
Open questions for the critic:
                   - Does stillness healing the relief read as memory?
                   - Is the melt psychedelic because it's liquid TIME, or
                     just noisy geometry? Name it in the critique.
Unchanged from base (subtraction guard): projector, beam, rail, o11y ring,
source, palette. Deletion test: if the melt shader is removed the study
reduces exactly to wallform v8 — so the melt must be the only change.
