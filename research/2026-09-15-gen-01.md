# Study: gen-01 — generational loss as image (chain before code)

Direction: art-direction.svx 2026-09-13 "Generational loss as image"
(Joel-approved): rephotograph your own render through the screen plane
repeatedly; let the degradation be the picture; measure how many
generations until the source is gone.

## The chain

**Source finding:** the verified measurement ethos of the lane (clock-02's
delay REQUIRED visibility at 3s on slow 1943 footage; the local-eye pass
proved self-observation needs an outside check). The provocation makes the
measurement itself the artwork — generations until source-death is a
number the audience can watch being spent.

**Artistic question:** when the picture IS the count of its own losses,
does the audience watch the image or the countdown?

**Material:** the study's own previous frame — no external source at all,
or optionally seeded with the CC-PD clock film for one generation. Pure
self-mediation makes it the most "do-less" possible: no new material, no
new pulls, no ingest dependency.

**Three.js operation:** render-to-target ping-pong: frame n's output
becomes frame n+1's texture, reprojected through a slight (sub-pixel)
sampling offset and gamma drift — the имени rephotography. A visible
generation counter (o11y `gen01.generation {n}` per second, plus a small
burned-in numeral) makes the loss legible. Optional first-generation seed
from the 1943 clip gives the loss a subject to eat.

**Observable results:** the numeral counts up while the image degrades;
the "source is gone" threshold is a measurable generation count recorded
in the ledger (e.g. `source_gone_at: N`); replay of any generation is
deterministic (ping-pong state is fully captured by the two targets).

**Lineage:** orthogonal branch from clock-02's grammar (no delay ring
needed; the temporal claim is succession itself). Satisfies the
provocation headless-first: everything verifiable without a phone or a
live feed; the measurement is the content.

## Build note preserved (module withdrawn per AD seq 16, 2026-09-14)
- A reference implementation exists at `research/scaffolded/study-gen01.ts.bak`
  (moved out of src on the AD's stop-pre-building directive). The load-bearing
  discovery: TRUE rephotography needs the presented generation's pixels fed
  back as the next generation's texture — double-rendering the same scene is
  NOT rephotography. Mechanism: render → `readRenderTargetPixelsAsync` →
  `DataTexture` (SRGB) as screen map, with accumulating sub-pixel offset;
  source-death via luma on the same readback. Twin-01's reference sits beside
  it (`study-twin01.ts.bak`): twin runs its own `<video>` at 0.9x (layer API
  carries one texture by design), catch-up rate bump + stagger on resync.
