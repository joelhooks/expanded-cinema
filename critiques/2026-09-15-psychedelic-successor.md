## The move

Psychedelia, read through the apparatus doctrine, is **time rendered as
liquid material** — not a poster filter. Three canon anchors:

- **Jordan Belson** (*Allures* 1961, *Vortex* 1965 — the psychedelic chapters
  of Gene Youngblood's *Expanded Cinema*, 1970): abstract light that behaves
  like a cosmology; the screen is a depth, not a plane.
- **Liquid light shows** (USCO, the Whitneys, Fillmore projectionists like
  Glenn McKay): oil, water, and interference projected live — the image is a
  fluid that the film's energy disturbs.
- **Degraded ephemeral film** (Prelinger archive chemical copies): the
  future generations of the source ARE the picture.

All three are delay-and-feedback stories. The loop already owns the delay
grammar (clock-02 same-view ring, withhold-01 aperture). The proposal
combines provocation "the surface shows its past" with provocation
"generational loss as image":

## Study 1: `surface/melt-01`

- **Lineage:** branches wallform-01 (keeps the facet heap + live footage
  under the beam; changes facet behaviour from static shards to
  feedback-driven melt).
- **The one operation:** the panel samples its own frame N back and
  displaces itself with it, gain driven by the live clip's luma. Motion
  melts the relief; stillness heals it. The film is the fluid; the surface
  is the vat.
- **Why psychedelia:** real time-slice feedback of moving signal onto
  moving signal is EXACTLY what liquid light shows did to footage — the
  source's energy physically perturbs the medium that shows it. Colour
  arrives only from the source clip and its light in the room — no channel
  split, so the 13:05Z colour pause holds.
- **Material four (per doctrine):** palette = source clip's own hue +
  named fog colour (declared in the chain, source-cited); light = beam is
  the only lamp, fog with a body; geometry = dense instanced relief
  (wallform heap carried forward); node shader = one TSL feedback
  displacement material — luma-sampled self-displacement is where the
  study's operation lives.
- **Razzle dazzle check:** motion means the whole relief erupts — cuts
  detonate the surface (luma jumps → maximum displacement). That is a
  five-second stranger-stopper without any effect piled on.
- **Subtraction guard:** if the melt reads as "warp filter over a video",
  line 4 of the floor fails — the apparatus (projector, beam, heap, rail)
  must stay visible through it.
- **Variable ladder:** v01 feedback at N=8 fixed → v02 displacement gain →
  v03 N varies across the surface (left = now, right = two seconds ago) →
  open question for the critic: does stillness healing the relief read as
  memory, or does noise never leave?
