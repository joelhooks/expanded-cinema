## Chain
- **Source finding** (informal, this sketch is procedural-on-purpose):
  multiplied mediation — a work seeing its own past — is a core expanded
  cinema temporal device. No citation yet; treat the visual claim as
  interpretation, and mark `needs_source` before any public write-up.
- **Artistic question**: can a surface showing *its own delayed past* read as
  time, not as an echo effect?
- **Operation**: render the current frame to a texture each frame; map a
  delayed copy (ring of render targets, delay N=24) onto the projection
  screen; the knot receives the video texture as its *only* light (no key
  light anywhere in the scene).
- **Observable result to examine**: the screen should trail the knot; the
  knot's surface should carry moving image. If the trail is invisible at
  N=24, sweep N.
